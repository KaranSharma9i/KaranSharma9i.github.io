About Me

Hi, I'm Karan — a data enthusiast with a scientific mindset.

I hold a Bachelor's degree in Science and I'm currently pursuing my Master's in Physics, where I developed a deep appreciation for statistical modelling and quantitative analysis. This academic foundation drives my approach to data — methodical, curious, and evidence-based.

I enjoy the full data lifecycle: from cleaning messy datasets and writing efficient SQL queries to building interactive dashboards in Power BI and Tableau. My programming toolkit includes Python with Pandas, NumPy, and SciPy for analysis, alongside Plotly, Matplotlib, and Seaborn for compelling visualisations.

I'm actively seeking opportunities as a Data Analyst, Business Analyst, or Data Scientist where I can apply inferential statistics, create data-driven narratives, and help organisations make smarter decisions.
